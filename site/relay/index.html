The mesimon relay

Your agents on your phone.

What you get

  • Your agents on your phone. Pair a phone browser at remote.mesimon.dev to watch your agents work, answer a permission or a question, send a prompt and file a ticket. It is end-to-end encrypted: the relay carries your board and cannot read it.
  • Coming soon: board sharing. One board on every teammate's computer, on the same subscription.

Price

$5 a month or $48 a year, per computer.

Phones and paired browsers are free. One key is one computer, and you can move it to another computer from your Polar customer portal.

Buy a key

After you buy

  1. Your license key arrives by email, and it is in your Polar customer portal.
  2. In mesimon, press Esc, open Sharing, then Remote Control, and choose Sign in. The relay field already reads relay.mesimon.dev:443.
  3. Paste the key in the License key dialog. Then Enable this board, choose Pair a browser and scan the code with your phone.

If your subscription lapses, a paired phone keeps its live view, and a ticket it files while your computer is away waits until you enter a new key.

Run your own

The relay also runs on your own server, and a relay you run needs no key.

Terms and privacy

  • Seller. Polar Software, Inc. sells the subscription as merchant of record and handles payment, tax and invoices. Its Buyer Terms, refunds included, and its Privacy Policy apply to your purchase.
  • Cancelling. Cancel any time in your Polar customer portal. It takes effect at the end of the period you paid for.
  • What the relay stores. Your encrypted board records; routing metadata, meaning which computers belong to which boards and in what role; each computer's public keys and the display name you sign in with; and Polar's id for the seat your key holds on that computer, never the key. The relay names the seat to Polar by the computer's device id. What a phone leaves for a computer that is away is deleted after 30 days. To have your computer and its boards removed, write to support.

  • Alpha. The service is in alpha and may change.
